Take it to the crag

Meet the Crag Ring.

The Gear Board keeps the rack at home; the Crag Ring carries it. A light 7 mm ring with 11 closed windows and a hand-size carry handle: rack your cams, grab it like a bag and walk in, then clip the handle to a sling or a tree at the base.

  • 11 fully enclosed windows: nothing slides off on the approach
  • Carry handle doubles as the hang point, on a hook, sling or tree
  • About 72 g and 7 mm thin: it rides in a pack, a duffel or the trunk

Material & specs

Printed, not molded.

Every Gear Board is FDM-printed in premium PLA+ — a durable, plant-based bioplastic with a soft matte finish. Layer lines are part of the look, not a defect: it's a 3D-printed object and we'd rather you know that going in.

Dimensions200 × 169 × 9 mm (7.9 × 6.7 × 0.35 in), W×H×D
