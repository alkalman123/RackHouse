Questions

Risks & honest answers

What if the goal isn't reached?

Kickstarter is all-or-nothing. If the campaign doesn't reach $1,200, no card is charged and the shop keeps running at regular prices.

What could delay my reward?

Mainly print volume. If more people back it than one printer can handle on schedule, overflow orders go to a professional print farm that prints the same files in the same materials. Backers get an update every two weeks either way.

Why closed windows instead of hooks?

Because gear moves. A rack gets carried to the crag, tossed in a trunk, stuffed in a pack or bumped in a closet, and an open hook or notch lets a carabiner ride right off. A closed window can only be left by opening the gate again. Every organizer was checked in CAD for that: each window is its own closed opening, wide enough for a carabiner and with a strip thin enough to clip around.

Is any of this climbing safety gear?

No. They are gear organizers, and each one says NOT FOR CLIMBING right on it. None of them is rated to hold a person.

Can I back from outside the US?

The first campaign ships to the US only, to keep shipping costs and timelines honest. International backers can pledge the $5 Spotter tier, and international shipping comes once the shop is up and running.

I'd rather just buy one now.

You can: the shop is open at regular prices. The campaign is the cheapest way to get them, and it's what funds the next designs.

Be first in line for the early birds.

Early-bird tiers are limited to the first 50 backers. Leave your email and you'll get one message on launch day, nothing else.
