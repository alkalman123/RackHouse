# Prototypes, round 2: enclosed openings only (R11–R20, B11–B20)

Following your feedback that **R05** and **B06** were the only usable
ones: every clip point here is a **fully enclosed opening** (a closed
loop, window, slot or box), so a clipped carabiner can't fall off in any
direction. **Nothing is on the website yet.** Pick your favorites after
testing.

`rings2.jpg` and `bars2.jpg` show all twenty. Each part has its ID
debossed on it.

## What changed from round 1

- **Closed openings only.** No open hooks, notches or bare rails.
- **Made to travel.** These are flat, rounded plates with no wall posts,
  so nothing snags in a pack or rattles in a car. Each one has its own
  closed hang eye, slot or handle. That hangs on a closet hook, a car
  grab handle or headrest, a pack haul loop, or an anchor sling at the
  crag.
- **Checked on every part by `designs/prototypes2.py`:**
  - each clip opening is a separate closed hole at least 14 mm across,
    so the carabiner wire passes through;
  - the strip of material the carabiner closes around, from the opening
    to the outside edge or a big open window, is 12 mm or less, so it
    clips easily. Openings never share a strip with a neighbor;
  - every strip is at least 5 mm wide, for strength;
  - one solid, prints flat with no supports, fits a 210 mm bed.

## Printing

- Every file prints **flat as exported**, ID side up, **no supports**.
- 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 30% gyroid. PETG if it'll ride in a hot car;
  otherwise PLA+.
- All twenty use about 1.33 kg of filament.

## How to test each one

Score 1–5 and write notes. Use your own gear.

1. **Clip**: cam carabiners, a wiregate quickdraw and a locker. Does each
   one go through and close with one hand?
2. **Can't fall out**: clip a full load, then shake it, flip it upside
   down and swing it. Does anything come off?
3. **Closet**: hang it on a hook. Easy to grab a piece one-handed?
4. **Car**: hang it from a grab handle or headrest and drive a bumpy road.
   Any rattling or pieces jumping around?
5. **Pack**: stuff it in a pack, loaded, then take it out. Did it snag,
   and is everything still clipped?
6. **Crag**: clip it to a sling or tree. Is it easy to rack up from it?
7. **Load**: hang roughly twice your rack weight for 24 hours. Any
   bending or cracking?
