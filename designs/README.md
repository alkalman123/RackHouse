# Rackhouse product designs

These are the original Rackhouse Supply Co. designs for the four printed
products. Everything is generated by one parametric script, so you can
change any dimension and rebuild every file.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `rackhouse_designs.py` | Parametric CAD source for all four products |
| `print-ready/rackhouse-gatekeeper.stl` | Gatekeeper V3, flat on the bed |
| `print-ready/rackhouse-rock-ring.stl` | Rock Ring V3, face down |
| `print-ready/rackhouse-draw-bar.stl` | Draw Bar, face down |
| `print-ready/rackhouse-cup-cradle.stl` | Cup Cradle V2, stem down |
| `build-report.json` | Watertight/single-body checks, sizes, volumes |
| `make_usecase_svgs.py` | Builds the site's "in use" illustrations from the CAD outlines |
| `make_dimension_drawing.py` | Builds `img/rockring-dimensions.jpg` and `img/drawbar-dimensions.jpg` |
| `LICENSE` | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for the public; commercial rights reserved |

`models/*.stl` (used by the website's 3D viewer) are the same parts in
their use orientation.

## Rebuild

```
pip install manifold3d trimesh networkx matplotlib
python3 designs/rackhouse_designs.py
python3 designs/make_usecase_svgs.py
python3 designs/make_dimension_drawing.py
```

Every build checks that each part is a single, watertight solid (also
after reloading the exported STL the way a slicer does), that debossed
text stays clear of edges, and writes the result to `build-report.json`.

## Print settings

All four parts print with **no supports**. PLA+ is fine for indoor use;
use **PETG** for anything that will live in a hot car (the Cup Cradle,
or a Gatekeeper in a van).

| | Gatekeeper V3 | Rock Ring V3 | Draw Bar | Cup Cradle V2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Size (print orientation) | 209 × 104 × 10 mm | 184 × 206 × 26 mm | 200 × 44 × 20 mm | 101 × 101 × 135 mm |
| Bed needed | 210 mm in one direction (Ender 3, Prusa MK3/MK4, Bambu A1/P1/X1) | 210 mm in one direction (same printers) | 200 mm | any |
| Orientation | Flat, text up (as exported) | Face down, feet up (as exported) | Face down, standoffs up (as exported) | Stem down (as exported) |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm | 0.2 mm | 0.2 mm | 0.2 mm |
| Walls | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Infill | 30% gyroid | 40% gyroid | 30% gyroid | 15–20% gyroid |
| Est. weight | ~65–85 g | ~110–140 g | ~55–65 g | ~140–180 g |
| Rated for | — | 8 kg of gear | 5 kg of gear | — |
| Notes | Brim optional | Smooth face on the bed; the numbers and text are debossed into it | Same as the Rock Ring | Ribs flex a little: 4 walls makes the fit tighter |

## Mounting

The Rock Ring (one) and Draw Bar (two) hang on #8 pan-head screws
(head up to 10 mm) through keyholes. Drive each screw into a stud or a
rated drywall anchor and leave the head about 9 mm out from the wall.

## Key dimensions (change them in the script)

- **Gatekeeper:** 10 mm thick, 22 mm frame, four 20 × 11 mm gear slots at
  30 mm pitch, 13 mm hang hole, J-hook with a 23 mm throat and a 9 mm
  gate angled at 55°. Rail is debossed "RACKHOUSE" and "NOT FOR CLIMBING".
- **Rock Ring:** 184 mm ring, 20 × 14 mm band, 15 numbered notches on
  the inner edge 18° apart (6 mm deep, so the band is 14 × 14 mm where a
  carabiner clips on), coat-hook helmet hook with a 13 mm throat, keyhole
  for a #8 screw, three 12 mm standoff feet. Text: "RACKHOUSE" and
  "NOT FOR CLIMBING" on the top of the band.
- **Draw Bar:** 200 × 44 mm, 8 mm thick, seven 13 × 22 mm slots at 22 mm
  pitch above an 11 mm bottom rail, two keyholes 176 mm apart in 12 mm
  standoff ends. Text: "RACKHOUSE · NOT FOR CLIMBING".
- **Cup Cradle:** stem tapers from 58.6 to 62 mm, 8 ribs span 69 → 79 mm,
  45° flare, 94 mm cup (51 mm deep), 7 mm drain, three arch windows and
  one gate window.
