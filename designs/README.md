# Rackhouse product designs

These are the original Rackhouse Supply Co. designs for every printed
product. Everything is generated by parametric Python scripts, so you can
change any dimension and rebuild every file.

## Carabiner organizers (the main line)

Seven organizers, picked from two rounds of 20 prototypes. Every clip
opening is a **fully enclosed window**: a clipped carabiner can't slide
off in any direction, so they work on a wall, in a car, in a pack and at
the crag.

| File | What it is |

| `prototypes2.py`, `prototypes2/` | Round 2: 20 enclosed-window prototypes (R11–R20, B11–B20) and the opening checks |
| `products.py` | The seven production designs: the chosen prototypes with RACKHOUSE · NOT FOR CLIMBING debossed in |

| `print-ready/rackhouse-sport-board.stl` | Sport Board (prototype B12), flat on the bed |
| `print-ready/rackhouse-approach-bar.stl` | Approach Bar (prototype B18), flat on the bed |

Every build checks that each part is a single watertight solid (also after
reloading the STL the way a slicer does), that it fits a 210 mm bed, and
that every clip opening is its own closed hole, at least 14 mm wide, with
a 5–12 mm strip a carabiner gate can close around.

## Rebuild

```
pip install manifold3d trimesh networkx matplotlib pillow
python3 designs/products.py              # the seven organizers
python3 designs/make_product_drawings.py
python3 designs/rackhouse_designs.py     # Gatekeeper + Cup Cradle
python3 designs/make_usecase_svgs.py
python3 designs/make_site.py             # product pages + store data

# renders (needs Chromium; serve the repo root first)
python3 -m http.server 8790 --bind 127.0.0.1 &
python3 designs/render/gear.py
python3 designs/render/make_images.py
python3 designs/make_share_image.py
```

## Print settings

Everything prints with **no supports**. PLA+ is fine for indoor use; use
**PETG** for anything that will live in a hot car (the Crag Ring in a
trunk, the Pocket Bar in a glovebox, the Cup Cradle).

| | Organizers (all seven) | Gatekeeper V3 | Cup Cradle V2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Flat, text up (as exported) | Flat, text up (as exported) | Stem down (as exported) |
| Bed needed | Up to 207 mm in one direction (Ender 3, Prusa MK3/MK4, Bambu A1/P1/X1) | 210 mm | any |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm | 0.2 mm | 0.2 mm |
| Walls | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Infill | 30% gyroid | 30% gyroid | 15–20% gyroid |
| Est. weight | see table above | ~65–85 g | ~140–180 g |

**Load-test before you sell.** The capacities on the site ("a double rack
of cams, a set of nuts and six quickdraws" on the Gear Board, and so on)
come from the CAD, not from a test. Hang the listed gear on a printed one
for a week before listing it.

## Hanging

No keyholes or screws through the part. Boards and bars hang from two
24 × 12 mm slots (two hooks, two screws with washers, or a sling through
both); the rings hang from a 24 mm top eye, and the Crag Ring from its
76 × 26 mm handle.
