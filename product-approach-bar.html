The Approach Bar

Ultralight 6 mm quickdraw bar

$16.00

Seven closed slots on an ultralight 6 mm bar that weighs about 36 g. Rack your draws on it at home and throw it straight in your pack: it's thin enough to slide down the back panel.

At the crag, clip the end slots to a sling or your harness and hand draws out in order. Every slot is fully enclosed, so nothing falls off.

ColorInk
  • Every opening fully enclosed: clipped gear can't fall off
  • Made to order — ships in 2–4 business days
  • Free standard shipping over $60
Not climbing protection The Approach Bar is a gear organizer, not an anchor, and it says NOT FOR CLIMBING right on it. Never clip it into a climbing system or hang body weight from it.

Size200 × 40 mm, 6 mm thick (7.9 × 1.6 in)

Holds7 quickdraws

Closet, car, pack, crag

Closet: Two end slots: hooks, a sling, or a carabiner on your harness or pack.

Car: hang it from a grab handle or headrest with a carabiner. Gear stays clipped on bumpy roads because every opening is closed.

Pack: it's flat and rounded, so it slides in without snagging, and nothing comes unclipped in the pack.

Crag: clip it to a sling, a tree or your pack at the base and rack straight off it.

Material & care

Printed in PLA+. Wipe clean with a damp cloth. PLA+ softens above about 55 °C (130 °F), so if it'll ride in a car parked in the sun, ask for PETG in the order notes and we'll print it in that instead, same price.

Shipping & returns

Made to order, 2–4 business days before it ships. Full policy on the shipping & returns page.
